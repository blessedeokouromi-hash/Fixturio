About & policies

About Fixturio

Fixturio is a free football predictions and live-scores site covering hundreds of leagues worldwide. Every match page shows a predicted score, the confidence behind it and the key markets that matter: match result, over/under 2.5 goals, both teams to score and correct score.

Our predictions come from a model that blends team form, recent results, home and away records and bookmaker odds. A higher confidence means the model saw more agreement between these signals. Nothing more, nothing magic.

Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. Football is football: favourites lose, draws happen. Use Fixturio as a starting point for your own thinking, never as a promise of an outcome.

Responsible gambling

Fixturio does not offer betting. We show predictions and link to licensed bookmakers as advertising partners.

  • You must be 18 or older (or the legal age where you live) to bet.
  • Betting can be addictive. Only bet money you can afford to lose, and never chase losses.
  • Set a budget before you start, and stick to it.
  • If gambling stops being fun, take a break or seek help. In the UK visit begambleaware.org; elsewhere, search for a gambling helpline in your country.

Predicted scores and confidence bars are statistical estimates. They are not tips, not promises, and past accuracy does not guarantee future results.

Privacy policy

We keep data collection to a minimum.

  • On your device only: saved matches, your theme choice and similar preferences are stored in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and clearing your browser data removes it.
  • Advertising: Fixturio is free because of advertising partners (including Adsterra). These networks and their advertisers may set cookies or use similar technologies to show and measure ads, including personalised ones. You can usually opt out through your browser settings.
  • Affiliate links: links to our betting partner (1xBet) may include a tracking tag so the partner knows a visitor came from Fixturio. No personal data is passed to them by us.
  • Accounts (optional): creating an account is entirely optional. If you create one, we store only your email address and the list of matches you saved, so your saves can follow you across devices. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and never visible to us in readable form. You can sign out at any time, and can ask us to delete your account and its saved data at any time.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and page requested) for security and uptime.
